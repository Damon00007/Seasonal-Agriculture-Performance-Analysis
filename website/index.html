Key Findings & Recommendations

Seasonal profitability: Kharif had the highest average profit and profit margin, while Zaid recorded negative average profit.
Crop pattern: All eight analyzed crops showed higher average yield in Kharif than Rabi and Zaid.
Water efficiency: Zaid had the highest average water usage but the lowest water-use efficiency.
Regional variation: State and season combinations showed substantial differences in average profit.
Interpretation: Environmental conditions varied considerably by season, but individual environmental variables showed weak record-level linear correlations with yield.

Recommendations

Optimize irrigation scheduling and water management during Zaid.
Consider crop-season combinations in cultivation planning.
Investigate unusual profit observations instead of automatically treating them as errors.
Combine seasonal, crop, environmental, resource and economic indicators for decisions.
